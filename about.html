About Manhattan Film Festival

20 Years of Independent Cinema Excellence

Celebrating Independent Cinema. Empowering Independent Voices.

For nearly two decades, the Manhattan Film Festival (MFF) has stood as one of New York City's most enduring cultural institutions dedicated to independent cinema. Rooted in the creative heritage of America's filmmaking capital, MFF champions stories, artists, and audiences who push culture forward.

Our Mission: Cultural Impact Through Independent Film

The mission of the Manhattan Film Festival is to advance the cultural landscape through the discovery and elevation of bold, independent voices—from New York City and from every corner of the world. We believe that cinema is one of society's most powerful tools for reflection, innovation, and change. MFF exists not simply to screen films, but to shape conversations, build community, and cultivate the next generation of storytellers who influence the world.

A Legacy Within NYC's Independent Film Heritage

New York City has historically served as fertile ground for independent filmmakers—from its early arthouse theaters to the downtown movements that redefined American cinema. The Manhattan Film Festival proudly continues this lineage.

Our programming reflects the city's global identity, presenting a diverse slate of both American and international films that honor NYC's artistic roots while embracing the worldwide community of filmmakers who share their work with us.

20 Years of Indie Cinema in NYC

Our Journey Through Independent Film

2006

The Beginning

Manhattan Film Festival launches with a vision to create a filmmaker-first platform in the heart of NYC, screening 50 independent films in our inaugural year.

2010

Growing Recognition

MFF expands to include international submissions, welcoming filmmakers from 20+ countries and establishing partnerships with Cinema Village.

2015

Innovation Era

Launch of MFF Plus sponsored screenings program and introduction of new categories including Gaming/Interactive Media, expanding our definition of independent storytelling.

2020

Adapting & Thriving

Successfully pivoted to hybrid festival model, reaching global audiences while maintaining our NYC roots and supporting filmmakers during challenging times.

2026

20th Anniversary

Celebrating two decades of independent cinema with our largest program yet, featuring 500+ films from 50+ countries and special anniversary events throughout the festival.

Manhattan: A Global Cultural Advantage

Our location in the heart of Manhattan provides filmmakers and audiences with a global advantage that few festivals can match.

As one of the world's most influential creative capitals, Manhattan allows the festival to connect filmmakers with industry professionals, distributors, press outlets, and international audiences drawn to New York's cultural vibrancy.

This geographic position transforms MFF from a local festival into an international launchpad—where independent voices gain access to the resources, networks, and visibility needed to reach global audiences.

Why NYC Matters for Independent Film

  • Home to major distributors and sales agents
  • International press and media headquarters
  • Diverse, sophisticated film audience
  • Rich independent film history and community
  • Access to industry decision-makers
  • Global cultural influence and visibility

Primary Venue

Screenings: June 12-22, 2026
