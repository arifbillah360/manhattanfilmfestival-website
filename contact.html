Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I submit my film?

All film submissions are handled through FilmFreeway. Visit our Submit page for complete guidelines, deadlines, and submission link.

When will I hear back about my submission?

Filmmakers will be notified of their selection status approximately 2-4 weeks after the submission deadline for their category. All filmmakers will receive notification regardless of selection.

How can I become a sponsor?

We offer various sponsorship levels and benefits. Contact us at partnerships@manhattanfilmfestival.com or visit our Advertise page for more information.

Do you offer press credentials?

Yes! Media professionals can apply for press accreditation through our Press page. Applications open 60 days before the festival.

How do I book a sponsored screening?

Our MFF Plus program offers sponsored screening opportunities outside of the main competition. Visit our Book a Screening page for availability and pricing.

Are there volunteer opportunities?

Yes! We welcome volunteers during the festival. Please contact us using the form above and select "Volunteer Opportunities" as your inquiry type.
