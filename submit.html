Eligibility & Submission Rules

Eligible Films

  • Films completed after January 1, 2023
  • World, International, North American, US, and Regional premieres preferred but not required
  • All genres and formats accepted
  • Films of any country of origin
  • English language or English subtitled
  • Student films must include proof of enrollment

Technical Requirements

  • DCP (Digital Cinema Package) preferred for screenings
  • ProRes, H.264, or MP4 accepted for preview
  • Minimum resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
  • Aspect ratio: Any standard format
  • Audio: Stereo minimum, 5.1 preferred

What We Need

  • Completed online submission form
  • Screening link (Vimeo, YouTube, or similar)
  • Film stills (minimum 3 high-resolution images)
  • Director bio and filmography
  • Synopsis (short and long versions)
  • Director's statement (optional but recommended)
  • Press kit (if available)

Important Notes

  • No submission fee refunds or transfers
  • Multiple submissions allowed (separate fees apply)
  • Incomplete submissions will not be considered
  • Selected films must be available for festival dates
  • Filmmakers must secure all rights and clearances
